Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ArbiTracker is an app for tracking anything you choose. This policy explains what happens with the information you enter in it. In short: it stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Everything you create in the app (what you track, your recordings, goals, reminders, charts and settings) is stored only on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. ArbiTracker has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app has no internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or receive any of your data, because we never have it.

Backups

  • Save data: when you save your data to a file, the file goes where you choose (for example your phone or a cloud folder). You decide who can see it.
  • Android backup: if Android's own backup is switched on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup with Google. This is handled by Android and your Google account, not by ArbiTracker, and you can switch it off in your phone's settings.

Notifications

Reminders and random check-ins are scheduled on your phone. The app asks for permission to show notifications and, for exact times, to set alarms. Nothing about them leaves your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete anything inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so first.

Contact
